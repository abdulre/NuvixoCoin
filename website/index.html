Platform

Everything You Need
On One Chain

NuvixoCoin is more than a coin — it's a complete blockchain platform with built-in financial tools.

⚡

Proof of Stake

Energy-efficient consensus. No mining hardware required. Stake your NXC and earn transaction fees by forging blocks.

🏦

Asset Exchange

Issue and trade custom tokens directly on the NuvixoCoin blockchain without relying on any third-party exchange.

💱

Monetary System

Create on-chain currencies with configurable minting rules, reserve systems, and built-in exchange offers.

🔒

Encrypted Messaging

Send end-to-end encrypted messages between accounts directly on the blockchain. Permanent and uncensorable.

📋

Phasing

Advanced multi-signature and conditional transactions. Set approval conditions, time locks, and voting thresholds.

🔀

Shuffling

Built-in cryptographic privacy mixing for NXC and assets. Enhance transaction privacy without external tools.

🗳️

Voting System

On-chain governance and polls with flexible voting models. Let your community make decisions transparently.

🛒

Digital Goods Store

Buy and sell digital products directly on the blockchain with built-in dispute resolution and feedback.

🔌

Full API

Complete JSON API for developers. Build wallets, exchanges, explorers, and dApps on top of NuvixoCoin.

Get Started

Up and Running
in Minutes

No mining rigs. No complex setup. Just download, run, and start forging.

1

Download the Wallet

Download the NuvixoCoin wallet for your operating system. Windows, Linux, and macOS supported.

2

Create Your Account

Generate a secure passphrase in the wallet. Your account address starts with NXC-. Write your passphrase down safely.

3

Get NXC

Receive NXC from an exchange or another wallet. Your balance appears instantly once the transaction confirms.

4

Start Forging

Hold at least 1,000 NXC and start forging. Earn transaction fees passively every time you forge a block.
